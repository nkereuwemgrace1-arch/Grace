# STATE 13 — 5 Similar Channels to Study

Subscriber numbers are third-party estimates from analytics sites (late 2026) and vary by source.

1. **Business Casual** (~1.1–1.2M) — Animated business and industrial history documentaries (e.g., Rockefeller's oil empire). Learn: how original 2D animation plus narration carries a founder story; long-tail "empire" topics.
2. **Company Man** (~1.5–1.8M) — 10–12 min brand mini-documentaries, rise-and-fall and "why it failed" stories of household names. Learn: steady upload cadence (~1/week), sponsor integration, consumer-brand topic selection.
3. **MagnatesMedia** (~1.6–1.9M) — Founder biographies, corporate scandals, tech platform wars. Learn: dramatic storytelling and emotional framing of founders who started from nothing.
4. **Modern MBA** (~800K) — Business video essays and corporate strategy post-mortems built on filings and earnings calls. Learn: research depth that builds trust; strategy angles like "pathway to market".
5. **Logically Answered** (~900K) — Economics of tech and social media, business-failure post-mortems. Learn: tech-company topics (fits your Claude idea) and sharp "why did this happen" titles.
