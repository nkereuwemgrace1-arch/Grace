# STATE 11 — Thumbnails

Shared style lock (applies to every prompt): semi-realistic hand-drawn comic illustration, bold black ink outlines, cel shading, bright high-key daylight palette (sky blue, fresh green, warm beige), saturated brand-color accents only in icons and props, thin warm glow outline separating the main figure from the background, 16:9 at 1280x720, bottom-right corner kept clear for the duration stamp, text no more than 5 words.

Clone differentiator vs. source: the year-range sub-line is replaced by a red ribbon tag with a short hook phrase ("UNTOLD STORY", "HOW IT GOT EVERYWHERE"), and each title has one bold red accent word.
