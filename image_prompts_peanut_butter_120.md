# Image Prompts (120 scenes): The Shocking Good Effects of Peanut Butter After 60

**How these work:** every prompt opens with the same FRAME block, word for word, so the board, border, chalk tray, camera and lighting stay identical across the video. Only the ON THE BOARD part changes. The script decides whether a scene shows **TEXT** (the key line written in chalk) or a **DIAGRAM** (chalk drawings with short labels).  
**Scenes:** 120 (88 diagram, 32 text). They cover the full script, each breaking at the end of a sentence (timestamps are estimates at 2.7 words per second).

**The constant FRAME block (used in every prompt):**

> Photorealistic photograph of a classroom chalkboard, shot straight-on at eye level so the board fills the entire 16:9 frame. The board is matte black slate with faint ghosted half-erased writing and light chalk smudges, framed by a worn natural-wood border. A wooden chalk tray runs along the bottom edge holding a few white chalk sticks and a grey felt eraser. Soft, even classroom lighting with no glare. All chalk is soft white; sky-blue chalk is the only accent color.
